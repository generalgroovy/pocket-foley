Sound ingredients

Mix a tone, a wash of noise and a short click. Softness smooths the start and lets the tail linger. Pitch movement bends the tone up or down over the sound.

Project files & sound format

WAV downloads contain the exact mono 44.1 kHz, 16-bit samples used for playback. Your device still controls listening volume. Nothing records your microphone or connects to hardware.

Projects save in this browser when possible. A project download keeps editable settings; a WAV keeps only the resulting sound. Undo remembers 30 edits in this session. Changing a setting or hiding this tab stops playback.
